## 3. Title → thumbnail psychology
- **The text rewrites the title**, never copies it.
  - The hypothetical "What if your X…" becomes a first-person exclamation ending in **"?!"** (7/7): "MY DAD IS THE NOOB KING?!", "I HACKED ROBLOX?!", "MY PET ATE MOM?!", "MOM PLAYS ROBLOX?!", "1 BLINK = 1 ROBUX?!", "10 SECONDS?!".
  - It drops "What if" and turns the idea into a shocked claim.
- **What the title gives:** the premise.
- **What the thumbnail adds:**
  1. the emotion (whose face reacts and how);
  2. the **proof detail**, circled in red (6/7): the clock, the wink, frozen mom, the username, the skin, the legs;
  3. sometimes the consequence (frozen mom, the lost skin).
- **What stays unanswered:** how it ends, and who wins. Nothing in the thumbnail shows the final outcome.
- **The text carries the premise; the face and the circled object carry the hook.** The background only sets the mood colour.
- **Recurring (7/7):** "?!", two-line text at the top, a radial-burst background, the kid avatar present, white sticker outlines.
- **Occasional:** a red curved arrow (2/7), the face-only layout (2/7), sound-word stickers like DING!/GULP! (2/7), a counter or UI chip (3/7).

## 4. Visual DNA
**Composition**
- Text block in the top 12-30% of the panel.
- Main subject centred in the middle 30-85%, large (60-90% of the panel width).
- The secondary character (the kid) sits **bottom-left** at about 35-45% scale, overlapping the panel edge (4/7).
- No empty space: the burst fills the background.

**Characters**
- The same cast as the videos: blocky avatars with large faces.
- The **kid ("you")**: brown bowl hair, red-orange shirt with a white lightning bolt, sometimes a headband. Present in 7/7, shocked in 5/7.
- Antagonists: mom (curlers, lavender robe, pearls, bunny slippers), the noob dad-king, the friend, the pet.

**Expressions**
- The antagonist is smug or angry.
- The kid is shocked (O-mouth, white eyes, sweat drops, shake lines), or proud when he holds the power.
- Eyes are often recoloured for emphasis: red (angry), green or cyan glow (power).

**Outline**
- A **thick white sticker stroke** around every character and prop (7/7), over the dark line art.
- So the stroke version is the default here; a `--no-stroke` variant is not used.

**Colour**
- Saturated radial bursts, one dominant hue per thumbnail:
  - orange-red: time and money;
  - green: hacking or power;
  - blue: friend or sad;
  - purple-pink: family, royal or absurd.
- Accents: gold (coins, crowns), red (circles and arrows), white.
- A soft centre glow behind the subject; no dark corners.

**Lighting**
- Flat cartoon shading with top-left highlights, plus a bright glow behind the subject.
- High contrast between the subject and the burst, because of the white stroke.

**Typography**
- 2-5 words in **ALL CAPS**, on 2 lines, in a chunky rounded display font (Luckiest-Guy-like).
- Line 1 is smaller: white, pale pink or yellow.
- Line 2 holds the payoff: bigger and **gold-gradient**, or a role colour (green for robux and hack, pink for mom).
- Each line has a thick brown or dark-red outline, a white outer stroke and a drop shadow, with a slight tilt or arc.
- The punch word is the biggest ("10", "MOM").

**Storytelling**
- Conflict (parent vs kid, power vs consequence), plus visible stakes (a timer, coins, a lost skin).
- One absurd contradiction: a disguise, mom's legs in a pet, mom as a gamer.
- Exaggeration: giant scale, glowing eyes, sweat.

## 5. Layout families (formulas)
**A. "Antagonist + shocked kid"** (refs 1, 4, 5, 6, 7)
- Use for a family member or creature doing the absurd thing.
- The antagonist is big in the centre and holds or shows the proof object; the kid is shocked, bottom-left or bottom-centre.
- Text: `MY <WHO> <DID THING>?!` or `<WHO> <VERB>?!`.
- Burst colour by mood. A red circle on the proof object, plus an optional arrow from the kid's eye-line.

**B. "Giant kid face + proof"** (refs 2, 3)
- Use for "What if YOU got/could…" power titles.
- The kid's face fills 70-90% of the panel width, with an expression matching the power (wink/grin, evil grin).
- A small proof element: a coin plus "+1", or an inset of the consequence with a UI bar.
- Text: `1 <ACTION> = <REWARD>?!` or `I <DID IT>?!`.
- A red circle on the proof; an optional curved arrow from the proof to the UI.

**Choosing:**
- The subject of the title is a family member, friend or pet → **A**.
- The subject is "you" with a power or rule → **B**.
- A **time limit** title → A with the antagonist holding a timer (ref 1).

## 6. Rules
**Do**
- Work at 9:16 (1080×1920).
- Text 2-5 words, ALL CAPS, 2 lines, ending "?!".
- One subject large; the kid present.
- A white sticker stroke on everything.
- One radial burst colour; one red circle on the proof detail.
- Every element readable at phone size: text at least about 9% of the panel width in letter height.

- Every thumbnail is Roblox: money = the **Robux icon** (`sets.robux`), proof objects from the Roblox UI (Buy Now prompt, Reset Character, a "Disconnected" dialog, a player list). Never the official ROBLOX logo.

**Don't**
- No full title as text, and no "What if" in the text.
- No more than 2 characters plus 1 prop focus.
- No dark or busy backgrounds, and no small text.
- Never show the ending.
- Never use the reference channel's exact characters, compositions or text: our cast is in `animator/style.py`.
- Never promise a result the video does not deliver.

## 7. Workflow for a new title
1. **Understand the title:** subject, situation, stakes, curiosity gap, the visual contradiction. Pick family **A** or **B**.
2. **Design:**
   - the subject and its expression;
   - the kid's reaction and place;
   - the proof object to circle;
   - the burst colour;
   - the text (rewrite the title into a 2-5 word "?!" line) and its colours.
3. **Generate:** draw it in code with the video cast, **only at 9:16, 1080×1920**. Also give an image-generation prompt if the user wants to use an AI image tool.
4. **QC against this list:**
   - it matches the title;
   - the style follows this DNA;
   - it reads instantly;
   - the subject is big;
   - the text is readable on a phone;
   - colours and contrast match;
   - it is curious but not misleading;
   - it is original.

## 8. 16:9 wrap (only if the user explicitly asks)
The references show it this way because YouTube displays Shorts like this. Take the 9:16 panel and scale it to 720 px high (404 px wide), placed at x 438. Fill the background with the same panel scaled to 1280 px wide, centred, Gaussian-blurred (radius ~20) and darkened by ~15%.

### 9.2 Long-thumbnail DNA (what all 3 share)
- **Text:** 1-2 lines, top-centre or top-left, filling the top ~30 %.
  - The word **ROBLOX** is always in it, and the payoff line ends with "?!" ("THE WORLD IS / ROBLOX?!", "SUCKED INTO / ROBLOX?!", "ROBLOX WAR?!").
  - Line 1 is smaller, white with a dark navy/brown outline. "ROBLOX?!" is the biggest: gold gradient, brown outline, white outer stroke, drop shadow.
- **Subject:**
  - The kid (or the 2 rivals) **big**: the head is 30-45 % of the frame height, cut at the chest or waist, never shown whole.
  - Placed on the left or in the middle, with **shocked O-mouth + sweat drop** (2/3) or **angry shout vs deadpan** (VS).
- **The premise is shown as a spectacle, mid-action:**
  - the world splitting into Roblox;
  - a vortex sucking him into a tablet;
  - two armies.
- **Story easter eggs (2/3):** small details from the video (the cursor, the tutu, the bunny ears, Mom's face, "HELP!").
- **No red circle (0/3):** motion FX lead the eye instead: a click ring, a swirl, a VS burst, flying cubes, speed lines, sparkles.
- **Background:** a full illustrated scene (a street, a battlefield, a tablet screen), not only a burst.
  - Bright day or sunset.
  - A split (real vs Roblox) when the title is about crossing over.
- **Look:** the same as the Shorts: a thick white sticker stroke on characters, props and text, flat shading, saturated colour.
- **Safe area:** keep text and faces out of the **bottom-right ~15 %** (the YouTube duration badge). Keep the text inside the top ~32 %.

### 9.3 Title → long-thumbnail text
- Drop "What if". Make a 2-4 word statement or question with ROBLOX:
  - "What If the WHOLE WORLD Became Roblox" → "THE WORLD IS ROBLOX?!";
  - "What If You Got Sucked INTO Roblox" → "SUCKED INTO ROBLOX?!";
  - a rivalry → "ROBLOX WAR?!" plus two name labels.
- Ours, for example: "What If the WHOLE SCHOOL Became Roblox?" → **"SCHOOL IS / ROBLOX?!"**.

### 9.4 Layout families (long)
- **L1 "Crossover split"** (ref 1): the kid in the middle, the background split real | Roblox, and his body turning into blocks where it crosses the seam. Add one story easter egg.
- **L2 "Pulled into the screen"** (ref 2): the kid big on one side, a device or portal on the other with the consequence visible inside it, and a swirl between them.
- **L3 "VS"** (ref 3): two rivals, a VS burst, armies behind, name labels at the bottom.
